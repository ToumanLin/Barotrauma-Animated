To remove underwear(bra and pants) when wearing clothes you need to add:
       <!--Hide Underwear v2--> <StatusEffect type="OnWearing" target="Character" disabledeltatime="true" interval="0.5" stackable="false"> <Affliction identifier="hideunderwear" amount="10.0" MultiplyByMaxVitality="true"  /> </StatusEffect><!-- --> <StatusEffect type="OnWearing" target="Character" disabledeltatime="true" interval="1" duration="1" stackable="false" > <ReduceAffliction identifier="hideunderwear" amount="0.038" MultiplyByMaxVitality="true" /> </StatusEffect>
Add this only to items where you can actually see underwear, since it adds some minor load on server.

I tested it with 20 characters with same clothes and no items in inventory. 
Update rate at ms:
1.81 no status effects at all.
1.85 with hide underwear.
Its not much, but its never enough of optimisation. 




Same method applies for husk infection and diving suits.
      <!--Hide hidehusklimbs v2--> <StatusEffect type="OnWearing" target="Character" disabledeltatime="true" interval="0.5" stackable="false"> <Affliction identifier="hidehusklimbs" amount="3.0" MultiplyByMaxVitality="true"  /> </StatusEffect>



To prevent some textures have white outlines, cover all sprites edges with small very transparent brush (for example 20% opacity and 20% flow).


To make patch weight less(if you are using photoshop), you can save png with 8bit compression. 
This can save from 2 up to 4 times in space, but at the end, it can create some white pixels on edges, 
which you still can paint over.
(Export>export As>PNG>"smaller file size 8-bit")









Diving suits also has unique breathing system, add it to "wearable":
        <!-- play underwater breathing sound outside water -->
        <StatusEffect type="OnWearing" target="Character" interval="5" comparison="And">
          <Conditional InWater="false" />
          <Conditional IsDead="false" />
          <Sound file="%moddir%/content/sounds/DivingSuitBreath1.ogg" range="250" volume="1.1" selectionmode="Random" />
          <Sound file="%moddir%/content/sounds/DivingSuitBreath2.ogg" range="250" volume="1.1" />
          <Sound file="%moddir%/content/sounds/DivingSuitBreath3.ogg" range="250" volume="1.1" />
          <Sound file="%moddir%/content/sounds/DivingSuitBreath4.ogg" range="250" volume="1.1" />
        </StatusEffect>
        <!-- shish -->
        <StatusEffect type="OnWearing" target="limb" targetlimb="head" interval="5" duration="0.5" comparison="And">
          <Conditional InWater="true" />
          <Conditional IsDead="false" />
          <Sound file="%moddir%/content/sounds/DivingSuitBreath1.ogg" range="250" volume="1.1" selectionmode="Random" />
