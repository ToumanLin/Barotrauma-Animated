Method to change namelist:
Copy the namelist that you want to use, and then paste it to this address: Content/Characters/Human
p.s. If you want to use chinese namelist, please aware that there are two diffrent versions. "names1" is the chinese version of the vanilla namelist (defult mod setting),
      but I add some new firstnames and familynames to it. "names2" is the namelist from [EA-HI]木卫二萌化计划 (In Engish it is known as [EA-HI] Animated Barotrauma)
      Please delate the number before use the namelist


改变角色名称生成清单的方法：
将你所希望使用的姓名清单复制到如下地址：Content/Characters/Human
注：中文名单有两份，names1为原游戏自带姓名名单的汉化版本，本人也添加了一些名字与姓氏（模组默认使用）。names2为木卫二萌化的姓名生成清单。使用前请删去文件名中的数字。


Remember to delete hash and steamworkshopid in filelist. All adjustments should be made to your own local copy.
所有改动应在模组的本地副本中完成，并确保删除fileslist中的hash和steamworkshopid
